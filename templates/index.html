About

Margaret Kyerematen-Debrah ACMA CGMA

Managing Director, Vertex Capital Africa

Margaret is a capital markets professional turned Pan-African operator with over 15 years across J.P. Morgan, Barclays Investment Bank, Nomura, and Jefferies - spanning Fixed Income, Investment Banking, and Treasury.

Margaret believes Africa's growth story lies in the development of its private sector, with support from the public. Through Vertex Capital Africa, she is focused on helping early and mid-stage businesses raise capital, achieve investor readiness, and build the structures needed to scale - bridging the gap between African ambition and global capital.

She is also the CEO and Co-Founder of EATO, a media and e-commerce platform connecting African food, culture, and community to a global audience
